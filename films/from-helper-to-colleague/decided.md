# Decided: From helper to colleague

The series' own decisions, and the module's that shape it. With each film's rows (`<n-film>/decided.md`), they make the *Decided* table in [proposal.md](proposal.md): run `python site-tools/build_series.py` after adding a row. A decision a film's thread makes, even one for the whole series, goes in that film's file, so threads never edit the same lines.

| 5 November 2026 | The spine is the plan's five stages and their gates: 1 *Suggests*; 2 *Acts for you, step by step*; 3 *Acts for you, within limits*; 4 *A contributor that proposes*; 5 *A contributor with a mandate*.
